**1.** Which question can whole-genome sequencing answer that standard culture + AST cannot?
a) Which antibiotic should this patient receive today
b) Whether isolates from several patients are the same strain
c) Whether the organism is Gram-positive
d) Whether the patient has a fever

**2.** Do you need to own a sequencer to do genomic surveillance? (Yes / No) Briefly explain why: ______________

**3.** Name two bacterial species that commonly cause surgical site infections: ______________

**4.** An Illumina sequencing run typically produces reads that are:
a) 100–300 bases long and highly accurate
b) 10,000+ bases long
c) the whole genome in one read
d) protein sequences

**5.** Which technology is better at showing whether a resistance gene is on a plasmid?
a) Illumina short reads alone b) Oxford Nanopore long reads c) AST d) MLST

**6.** A genome is 5 million bases. You have 1 million reads of 150 bases. What is the approximate coverage?
a) 3× b) 30× c) 150× d) 300×

**7.** What does a FASTQ file contain that a FASTA file does not?
a) Sequence names b) DNA letters c) Quality scores d) Gene names

**8.** After assembly, which result usually means a *better* assembly?
a) More contigs and a smaller N50
b) Fewer contigs and a larger N50
c) A total length twice the expected genome size
d) No contigs

**9.** The gene `mecA` in *S. aureus* indicates:
a) Vancomycin resistance b) MRSA (methicillin resistance) c) Carbapenem resistance d) A virulence toxin

**10.** `blaNDM-1` is an example of:
a) An ESBL b) A carbapenemase c) An efflux pump d) A housekeeping gene

**11.** The genome shows no known resistance gene, but AST says "resistant". Give one possible explanation: ______________

**12.** Two *Klebsiella* isolates are ST15 and ST307. Could they be part of the same recent transmission chain?
a) Yes, very likely b) No, they are different strain types c) Only if they are both ESBL

**13.** On a phylogenetic tree, what tells you how genetically different two isolates are?
a) Their order from top to bottom b) The branch lengths between them c) The colour of the labels

**14.** Four isolates are 2–6 SNPs apart and the patients overlapped on the same ward. This most strongly suggests:
a) Unrelated infections b) Possible transmission/outbreak c) Contamination of the sequencer

**15.** List three pieces of metadata that must be recorded with every isolate for surveillance: ______________
