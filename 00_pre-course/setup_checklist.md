# Pre-Course Setup Checklist
**Send to participants 2 weeks before the course. Allow about 20 minutes. Please finish it before Day 1, because the labs cannot start without accounts.**

---

## 1. Your laptop ☐
- [ ] Any laptop (Windows, macOS, Linux or Chromebook). **No software installation is needed.**
- [ ] A recent version of **Google Chrome** or **Mozilla Firefox**
- [ ] Charger (we will be working all day)
- [ ] *Recommended:* 8 GB RAM or more, so several browser tabs run smoothly

## 2. Galaxy account ☐ (required)
Galaxy is a free website for analysing biological data.
1. Go to **https://usegalaxy.eu**
2. Click **Login or Register → Register here**
3. Use your **UGHE email address**
4. Confirm your email (check your spam folder)
5. Log in once to make sure it works

## 3. Pathogenwatch account ☐ (required)
1. Go to **https://pathogen.watch**
2. Click **Sign in** and create an account (or sign in with Google/Microsoft)

## 4. Microreact ☐ (optional)
- **https://microreact.org** works without an account. Sign in (e.g. with Google) only if you want to save projects.

## 5. Trainee team only: GitHub account ☐
If you are on the trainee team (see `resources/trainee_team_guide.md`):
1. Create a free account at **https://github.com** and send your username to the lead facilitator, who will add you to the course repository
2. Keep the half-day dry run (about 1 week before the course) and the half-day documentation sprint (within 2 weeks after) free in your calendar

## 6. Optional pre-reading (20 minutes) ☐
- `resources/glossary.md`: skim the definitions; you don't need to memorise them
- Velin L, et al. *Surgical site infections and antimicrobial resistance after cesarean section delivery in rural Rwanda.* Ann Glob Health 2021;87(1):77. https://doi.org/10.5334/aogh.3413 (open access)

## 7. Bring ☐
- [ ] Laptop + charger
- [ ] Your Galaxy and Pathogenwatch **passwords**
- [ ] *Optional:* a question from your own work that genomics might answer

---

### For facilitators: venue checklist
- [ ] **Internet:** at least 20 Mbps shared, tested with 30 people in Galaxy at the same time. Have a **mobile hotspot backup**.
- [ ] Power strips at every table
- [ ] Projector + HDMI/USB-C adapters; a second screen for live demos if possible
- [ ] Flip charts and markers (Lab 6 briefings), sticky notes (exit tickets)
- [ ] Printed handouts: labs 1–6, glossary, quiz (×2 per person), action-plan template
- [ ] **USB sticks** with offline copies of all datasets, pre-computed results and handouts (in case the internet fails)
- [ ] Shared Galaxy history `UGHE-SSI course – precomputed` ready and **shared by link** (see facilitator guide)
- [ ] Dry run completed ≥ 1 week before (see facilitator guide)
- [ ] Certificates printed
