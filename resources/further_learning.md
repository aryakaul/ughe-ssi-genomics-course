# Further Learning & Resources
*Links checked 6 October 2026.*

## Suggested learning pathway after the course
| Step | What | Time | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Repeat Labs 2 and 4 on a **different** dataset | 3 h | Your Galaxy account; datasets in `00_pre-course/datasets.md` |
| 2 | Software Carpentry **Unix Shell** lesson | 4 h | https://swcarpentry.github.io/shell-novice/ |
| 3 | GTN **Hybrid assembly** (Unicycler, *E. coli* Illumina + Nanopore) | 4 h | training.galaxyproject.org → Assembly → "Unicycler assembly" |
| 4 | GTN **Bacterial genome annotation** (Bakta, PlasmidFinder, integrons) | 3 h | training.galaxyproject.org → Genome Annotation → "Bacterial genome annotation" |
| 5 | Analyse a real African outbreak dataset (Malawi *K. pneumoniae*, subsample about 15 isolates) | 1 day | Cornick et al. 2021, see `datasets.md` |
| 6 | Formal course (applications are competitive; bursaries are often available) | 1–2 weeks | Wellcome Connecting Science, Africa CDC |
| 7 | Run a real local project with your action plan | Ongoing | Monthly data clinic |

## Courses & networks in Africa and beyond
| Organisation | Relevance | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Africa CDC – Institute of Pathogen Genomics (Africa PGI)** | Continental pathogen genomics network, training and sequencing support | https://africacdc.org/institutes/ipg/ |
| **Wellcome Connecting Science** | Hands-on courses, many run in Africa (e.g. bacterial genomics, AMR), often with bursaries | https://coursesandconferences.wellcomeconnectingscience.org |
| **H3ABioNet** | Pan-African bioinformatics network with an online training history. *Its website was unavailable when checked in October 2026; check its current status.* | https://h3abionet.org |
| **PHA4GE** (Public Health Alliance for Genomic Epidemiology) | Standards for metadata, typing and AMR reporting | https://pha4ge.org |
| **WHO GLASS** | Global AMR surveillance system and reporting | https://www.who.int/initiatives/glass |

## Data standards (for your own projects)
- PHA4GE **minimal contextual data** specification: https://github.com/pha4ge/minimal-pathogen-agnostic-contextual-data-specification
- PHA4GE **genotyping (typing) specification**: https://github.com/pha4ge/genotyping-specification
- PHA4GE **hAMRonization** (harmonising AMR tool outputs): https://github.com/pha4ge/hAMRonization
- Public read archives: **ENA** https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena · **NCBI SRA** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra
